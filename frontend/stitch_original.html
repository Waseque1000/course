Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers regarding courses, access, monetization, and mentorship.

Can I enroll in both SEO Zero to Hero and Blogging Master? expand_more
Yes! Many learners take both or bundle them. The SEO track equips you to master technical search algorithms and rankings, while the Blogging Master track focuses intensely on editorial velocity, affiliate partnerships, and high-CPM display ad networks.
Do I need prior coding or technical skills to start? expand_more
No technical background is required. We teach you step-by-step from domain setup and WordPress/Ghost configuration to no-code schema deployment, standard HTML markup, and automated AI assistance.
How does the 1-on-1 mentor review process work? expand_more
At key project milestones (such as your keyword map, site architecture audit, or capstone affiliate article), you submit your work to our instructor review portal. You receive structured video teardowns and written feedback within 48 hours.
Is there a refund policy if it's not right for me? expand_more
We provide a 14-day, 100% money-back guarantee. If you complete the initial modules and feel the curriculum doesn't provide tremendous practical value, simply email support for an immediate full refund.
